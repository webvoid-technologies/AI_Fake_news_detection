## Source Identification
The domain/platform and the author or page name, if identifiable.

## Domain Reputation

## Transparency Indicators
Bullet points: does the content show an author, a date, citations/links to sources, an about page, contact info — or are these missing?

## Limitations
Explain clearly what cannot be determined about the source's credibility from the URL and content alone. Never claim a source is trustworthy or untrustworthy without evidence.

## Verdict
One line: **Higher / Moderate / Low / Indeterminate** credibility signal, with a one-line justification.
